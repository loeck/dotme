BEHIND THE SCENE

A quiet little world.

A landscape made of tiny cubes, generated anew each time you visit. Light follows your local time, and the water responds to your pointer.

Made with

Three.js and WebGL for the landscape, water and atmosphere. A tiny, independent WebGL canvas brings the loading cubes to life.

Vite, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS for the site.

Sounds from nature

Optional field recordings from salilnair (water), Nox_Sound (wind), AlanCat (rain), Sclolex (insects), and VKProduktion (birds). Shared on Freesound under CC0; excerpts edited and softly mixed for this landscape.

Weather from the real world

Weather from the selected location, Paris by default, shapes the clouds, rain and wind. When unavailable, the landscape picks a random mood.

Weather adapted from Open-Meteo data, under CC BY 4.0. Conditions are interpreted for this miniature world.
